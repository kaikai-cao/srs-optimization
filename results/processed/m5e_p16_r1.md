**Overlap, not identity of peaks.** The two quantities overlap heavily: at the in-flight
peak the Worker WASM capacity is already at 94% of its final value. But their
individual maxima are NOT at the same instant -- the in-flight peak is at t=4210.3 ms,
while the capacity final value is only reached at the last grow t=6305.4 ms
(2.1 s later for p18; the gap is smaller but non-zero for p16).
M5-E said "the two peaks do not coincide" and retracted it; the precise correction is
"they coincide in being HIGH, but not at their respective maxima".

No residual is computed here. In-flight counts logical clone buffers; capacity is linear
address space; WS is resident pages. They are three different objects. The prohibition on
adding them together stands, but for the correct reason: clone bytes and the WASM copy are
*separate* memory objects that may coexist, capacity is not residency, and the current
telemetry cannot convert any of them directly into the Working Set (M5_PROBE_DESIGN 10.9).
